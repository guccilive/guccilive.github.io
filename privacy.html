Privacy policy

Office Commander is a personal dashboard used only by its owner, on their own Home Assistant server at home.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's YouTube account (scope youtube.readonly): playlists, liked videos, subscriptions and search results. It cannot change anything on the account.

How the data is used

Lists are fetched when the dashboard is opened, shown on screen, and used to start playback on the owner's TV. They are not stored, sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the owner's own devices.

Tokens

The Google sign-in token is kept only on the owner's Home Assistant server. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/guccilive.
